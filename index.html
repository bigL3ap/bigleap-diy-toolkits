How to review the toolkits

For Coby, Scott, Kristina, Shema and anyone else looking at the drafts before next Monday's call.

  1. Open the Business Profile first

    Click “Build your Business Profile” at the top. Answer as if you were a real prospect, or keep the example answers. Your profile is saved in this browser and fills in the other toolkits automatically.

  2. Follow the path it gives you

    The profile recommends which toolkits to do and in what order. Open one from the map or the list. Each toolkit opens with example answers, so you can see a finished plan right away.

  3. Change the answers and watch the plan update

    Results update as you answer. There is no submit button. Try edge cases from real clients: no website, an app, a franchise with ten locations, a B2B firm with HubSpot.

  4. Check the hand-offs

    At the bottom of every plan: “Copy my plan”, the “Talk to …” button for that service's owner, the next toolkit, and links to other services. Make sure each one goes somewhere sensible.

  5. Leave feedback where Dallas will see it

    Reply in the toolkit thread in Slack with the toolkit name and what to change. Owners: answer your items on the Open items tab so the drafts can move to v2.
