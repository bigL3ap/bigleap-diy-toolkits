Free toolkit FAQs

Are the toolkits really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no email gate. Use as many as you like.

Do you store what I enter?

No. Your answers stay in your own browser and are never sent to us. My Profile shows everything you've entered, and you can download it, move it to another browser or clear it anytime.

Where do I see my answers and progress?

In My Profile. It holds your Business Profile and every toolkit's answers, plus your journey, your roadmap and your progress. Open any toolkit again and your answers are already filled in.

Which tools do I need?

Mostly free ones: Google Tag Manager, GA4, Search Console, Google Business Profile and the free tier of HubSpot. Each toolkit says when something needs a paid plan.

What if I get stuck?

Every plan ends with a button to email the Big Leap expert who wrote it. Bring your plan and we'll pick up where you left off.
