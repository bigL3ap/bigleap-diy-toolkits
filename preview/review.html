Before the meeting: review in 10 minutes

  1. Build a Business Profile. Answer as a real prospect. It's saved in your browser and fills in the Paid Media, SEO, Digital PR, Email and RevOps 7 toolkits. RevOps 1–6 don't read it yet.
  2. Open your toolkits. Each opens with example answers, so you see a finished plan right away.
  3. Try edge cases. No website, an app, ten locations, a B2B firm on HubSpot. Results update live.
  4. Check the hand-offs. Copy my plan, the “Talk to …” button, Previous and Next, and the links to other services.
  5. Answer your open items below, and post toolkit feedback in the Slack thread with the toolkit name.
