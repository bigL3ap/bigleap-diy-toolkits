Free toolkit FAQs

Are the toolkits really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no email gate. Use as many as you like.

Do you store what I enter?

No. Your answers stay in your own browser. Your Business Profile is saved there too, so the next toolkit can fill itself in. Clear it anytime.

Which tools do I need?

Mostly free ones: Google Tag Manager, GA4, Search Console, Google Business Profile and the free tier of HubSpot. Each toolkit says when something needs a paid plan.

What if I get stuck?

Every plan ends with a button to talk to the Big Leap expert who wrote it. Bring your plan and we'll pick up where you left off.
