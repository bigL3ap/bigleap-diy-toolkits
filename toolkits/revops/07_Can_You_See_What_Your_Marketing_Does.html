Can You See What Your Marketing Does?

Eight quick answers about your website and ad accounts. You get your tracking build list in install order, with how-to steps for your platform and a test checklist.

The RevOps DIY path

    Your tracking today

    Example answers

    Not sure? The site footer often says "Powered by…", or ask whoever built it.

    Is Google Tag Manager installed?

    Tag Manager (GTM) is a free Google tool that holds all your tracking codes in one place, so you add them without editing the site each time.

    Is Google Analytics 4 installed?

    GA4 is Google's free website analytics. Its ID starts with G-.

    How was it added?

    Adding it two ways counts page views and events twice. We check for that.

    Is your site verified in Google Search Console?

    Search Console is free. It shows the Google searches that bring people to your site, and any problems Google has reading it.

    Do you get visitors from the EU, UK or EEA?
    Do you have a cookie consent banner?

    Consent Mode passes the visitor's Accept or Decline to your Google tags. Google requires it for ads and measurement in the EEA and UK.

    Which ad platforms do you run or plan to run?

    Each ad platform needs its own small piece of code (a "tag" or "pixel") on your site so it can learn who converts. Pick none if you don't advertise yet.

    Are those ad tags already on your site?
    How much do phone calls matter to you?

    If a call is how most people reach you, form tracking alone will miss most of your leads.

    Where do new leads end up?

    A CRM is the system that keeps your contacts and deals, like HubSpot.

              How we count this

              Core pieces are the ones every business that markets online needs: a place to manage tags (Google Tag Manager, or Shopify's own Google and Meta apps on Shopify), GA4, Search Console, and a CRM that keeps each lead's source. Consent Mode counts when you have EU, UK or EEA visitors (or aren't sure). One ad tag counts for each ad platform you run or plan. Call tracking counts when calls matter a lot.

              Missing means you answered No. Check means you answered Not sure or Some: it might already be done. Optional items are not counted.

              Time is a rule-of-thumb range for someone doing it for the first time, added up across missing and check items. DNS changes and ad platform reviews can add waiting time on top.

              Free tools and plan limits change. Check your own account before you build. Menu paths were checked in October 2026 and may move.
