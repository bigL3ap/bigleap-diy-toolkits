    Your Google Ads account

    Tracking basics
    Google Tag Manager on your site From your profile
    Google Analytics 4 (GA4) on your site From your profile
    GA4 linked to Google Ads
    Auto-tagging turned on

    Auto-tagging adds a click ID to every ad click so GA4 and your CRM know the visit came from an ad. It is one switch in Google Ads.

    Is there a conversion action that fires?

    A conversion action tells Google Ads what a win looks like: a form sent, a call, a purchase. Without one, Google cannot tell good clicks from bad.

    The page your ads will send people to
    One clear action to take
    Loads fast on a phone

    One clear action means one form, one button or one phone number to tap, not a menu of choices. Check speed free at pagespeed.web.dev.

            How we calculate this

            Each check is OK or a gap. "Not sure" counts as a gap until you check, because an unverified setting is the most common reason spend gets wasted.

            A gap wastes spend when ads could run but the money would be lost or unmeasured: no conversion that fires, auto-tagging off, no clear action on the page, a slow mobile page, a stale Business Profile, products not in Merchant Center, an app without measurement, or a budget too thin to learn. Account access and setup gaps block launch instead of wasting money, so they are listed but not counted in the red number.

            Rule of thumbValue

            Time estimates assume someone comfortable with the tools working on one site. They are ranges, not quotes.

            Free tools and plan limits change. Check your own account before you build. Nothing here is a promise of results; any number marked rule of thumb is a starting point, not a measurement of your account.
