    Your business

    Example answers

    Your name only shows on your profile card and stays in your browser. Some industries have special Google Ads rules, and we flag them.

    Who do you sell to?

    B2B means your buyers are other companies. B2C means your buyers are people buying for themselves.

    What do you sell?
    Can people buy online?

    An online checkout opens up Shopping ads. Shopify connects to Google in a few clicks.

    Do customers visit you in person?
    How far do your customers come from?

    Local is one city or metro. Regional is a few states or a province.

    Where do your leads and customers live?

    A CRM is the system that tracks each lead and sale, like HubSpot, Salesforce or Zoho.

    What should ads get you most?

    Pick the one result you would pay for. Leads means form fills. Awareness means more people knowing your name.

    $/ mo

    What you would pay Google each month, not counting agency fees. Leave blank if you are not sure yet.
