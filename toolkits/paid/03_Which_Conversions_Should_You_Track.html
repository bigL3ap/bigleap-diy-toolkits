Which Conversions Should You Track?

Answer a few questions about how your business makes money. You get a named event plan, which events should steer your ad bids, and a UTM naming starter.

The Paid Media DIY path

    How you win customers

    Example answers

    What counts as a win for you online?

    A conversion is an action that is worth money to you. Pick every one that applies. Page views and time on site are not wins.

    Who do you sell to?

    B2B sales usually take weeks after the first form. That changes which conversion your ads should learn from.

    Which CRM do you use?

    A CRM keeps your contacts and deals. It's how you tell your ad platforms which leads turned into sales.

    Which ad platforms do you run or plan to run?

    Pick none if you don't advertise yet. Your GA4 plan still works for SEO and email.

    What happens after someone sends your form?

    This decides how the conversion is counted. A thank-you page is the easiest to track.

    Where is your online store?

    Shopify and WooCommerce have apps that send purchase events for you.

    Is an app measurement SDK in your app?

    An SDK is a code library your developer adds to the app. It reports installs and in-app actions to analytics and ad platforms.

              How we calculate this

              Lead value = average first sale × (leads that buy ÷ 10). Example: a $5,000 first sale and 2 of 10 leads buying gives $1,000 a lead. It's an estimate to help ad platforms rank leads, not a forecast. Leave the fields blank and the plan passes no value.

              Primary conversions are the ones Google Ads bids toward. We suggest primary only for actions that are close to money and happen often enough for the platform to learn from. Everything else is secondary: you still see it in reports, but it doesn't steer bids.

              Qualified-lead imports become primary only once you have steady volume. As a rule of thumb, Google's automated bidding learns best with at least a few dozen conversions a month in a campaign. Check with your own account before switching.
