          How we calculate this

          The recommended flows depend on your kind of business. A flow counts as missing when it is recommended for you and you didn't tick it as running. Flows are ranked by how directly they lead to a sale, booking or conversation for that kind of business. Sunset always comes last: it earns nothing directly, but it protects your inbox placement.

          The headline estimate is a rule of thumb, not a forecast. For each missing flow: contacts on your list × the share who enter that flow in a month × the share of those who take the goal action, times your average value. Change the shares below to match your own numbers; the plan updates.
