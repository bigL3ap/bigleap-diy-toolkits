    Your Google Business Profile

    The listing with your map pin, hours and reviews that shows up when people search your name or "near me". It's free.

    Tick what's filled in on your profile (leave it if you're not sure)
    Are your name, address and phone the same everywhere?

    People call this NAP: name, address, phone. Matching details help Google trust that every listing is the same business.

    Where else are you listed and in control?
    Reviews Do you ask customers for a Google review?
    Do you reply to reviews?

    Never offer anything in return for a review, and never ask only happy customers. Both are against Google's rules.

            How we calculate this

            The headline counts the local basics still missing out of eight: a verified profile, a complete profile, matching name, address and phone, the main directories, asking for reviews, replying to reviews, location pages, and no competing profiles. "Haven't checked" counts as missing until you check.

            Times are rough first-time ranges. Items marked "per location" are multiplied by your location count. They are rules of thumb, not promises. Google's own waiting time (verification, edits under review) is extra.
