How Exposed Are You to AI Overviews?

Five quick answers about your content, right? You get an honest exposure read, plus the formatting changes that actually help AI Overviews and answer engines pull your pages into their answers.

The SEO DIY path

    Your content today

    Example answers

    What is your website built on?

    Used to point to the right menu for adding FAQ or HowTo structured data.

    Do your key pages answer the main question in the first sentence or two?

    Before any history, story or "why it matters" content. An AI tool usually lifts the first clear, complete answer it finds.

    Do you use FAQ-style sections, with headers phrased as questions?

    Headers written as real questions ("How long does X take?") give an AI tool a clean question-and-answer pair to lift.

    Do you have FAQPage or HowTo structured data on any pages?

    Structured data (schema) is hidden code that marks up a question-and-answer or step-by-step section so search and AI tools can read its structure directly.

    Do you currently rank on page 1 of Google for any of your core keywords?

    A rough proxy for topical authority. AI Overviews tend to draw from pages that already show real search strength, not formatting alone.

    How often is your core content reviewed or refreshed?

    A rule of thumb, not a rule: content that's never revisited tends to fall behind on facts, pricing or availability, which makes it a riskier source to quote from.
