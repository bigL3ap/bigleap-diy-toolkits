Can Google Find and Trust Your Site?

Eight quick answers about your website. You get a fix list for anything that stops Google or AI search from seeing your site, in the order to do it.

The SEO DIY path

    Your website today

    Example answers

    What is your website built on?

    Your fix list uses the menus for your platform. Not sure? Ask whoever built the site, or look at the login page you use to edit it.

    Used only on this page, to fill in the checks and code below. Nothing is sent anywhere.

    Is your site set up in Google Search Console?

    Search Console is Google's free tool that shows which pages Google has found and why others are left out. A sitemap is the list of your pages you hand to Google.

    Is your site in Bing Webmaster Tools?

    Bing's version of Search Console. Bing's index feeds Microsoft Copilot and is one of the sources ChatGPT search draws on.

    Open yoursite.com/robots.txt. What does it say?

    robots.txt is a small public file that tells bots where they may go. A leftover line from when the site was being built can hide the whole site.

    Search Google for site:yoursite.com. Do your important pages show up?

    "Important pages" means home, each main service or product page, About and Contact. The site: search is a quick look; Search Console's Pages report is the real count.

    Tick what's already true (leave it if you're not sure)
    Is Google Analytics 4 on the site?

    GA4 shows what search visitors do once they arrive. For SEO, GA4 plus a couple of conversion events is usually enough.

    About your site Roughly how many pages?

    Count blog posts and product pages too. A rough guess is fine.
