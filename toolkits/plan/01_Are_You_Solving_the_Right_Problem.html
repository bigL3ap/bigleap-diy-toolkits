Are You Solving the Right Problem?

Before you spend more time or money, check what kind of growth is actually on the table and what is holding you back. Answer what you know, skip what you don't, and add real numbers if you have them. You get a plain-English read of your situation, your top three priorities and the free toolkits that help.

Strategy starters · optional

Each one stands on its own. Do them in any order, or skip straight to a toolkit.

    Your situation

    Example answers

    Market situation

    What kind of growth is possible in your market. Pick what fits from what you know; “Not sure” is a fine answer, and we'll tell you how to find out.

    When someone looks for what you sell, how many credible options do they find?
    The number of people looking for what you sell is…
    When you have spent more on marketing, what happened?
    Of the people who could buy from you where you sell today, how many already do?
    Do people ask for things you don't offer, or from places you don't serve?
    How many new customers find you on their own: word of mouth, or searching your name?
    Have real numbers? Add them (optional)

    What you know from experience is enough. Real numbers make the read sharper, and each one fills in the matching answer above. Every field says where to find it with a free tool we trust.

    Business situation

    A business grows well when buyers can tell it apart, growth stays profitable and customers stick. These six show which of the three is holding you back.

    Why do customers pick you over the alternatives?
    How often do you discount to win a sale?
    As you've grown, the cost to win each new customer has…
    Do you know what a customer is worth over time, compared with what it costs to win them?
    How many customers come back, renew or buy again?
    How much new business comes from referrals and reviews?
    Have real numbers? Add them (optional)

    Your own sales records and free tools hold most of these. Each number fills in the matching answer above; you can still change it.

    Your situation, in one line

    Generatedemand: your most likely growth path

    Preliminary read. This comes from your answers, not from market research or your financials. Treat it as a starting point to check, not a verdict.

    Three lenses on your situation

    The market says what is possible, your business health says what is needed, and the three motions say what to do about it. They don't have to agree with each other.

    Business health

    A business is healthy only when all three hold. The one marked “Holding you back” becomes the objective.

      Every customer journey runs on three overlapping motions. They are not steps; all three run at once, each strongest at a different point. The deeper a motion has to go, the more groundwork it needs.
