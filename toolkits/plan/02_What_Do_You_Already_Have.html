What Do You Already Have?

Most businesses have more to build on than they think, and some of what they have can't carry the weight yet. Start with who buys from you and what drives them, then mark each foundation as usable, needs work, missing or not sure. You get credit for what works, a clear list of gaps, and what to fix first.

Strategy starters · optional

Each one stands on its own. Do them in any order, or skip straight to a toolkit.

    Your foundations

    Example answers

    How to mark each one
    • Usable: you have it, and it can carry what you want to build next.
    • Needs work: you have it, but it can't carry that yet.
    • Missing: you don't have it.
    • Not sure: you can't tell. That's useful too; we'll show you how to check.

    Owning something and being able to build on it are different. Each question says what “usable” means.
