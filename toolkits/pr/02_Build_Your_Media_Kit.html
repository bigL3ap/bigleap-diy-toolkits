          How the kit is written

          Every piece is filled in from your answers with fixed templates. Nothing is sent anywhere, and no AI writes it, so it reads plainly on purpose. The word counts are targets reporters and hosts commonly ask for (about 50 and 150 words), not hard rules.

          Anything you leave blank shows as a [bracketed placeholder], so you can see what to fill in before you send it.

          Free tools and plan limits change. Check your own account before you build. A media kit makes you easier to cover; it doesn't guarantee coverage.
