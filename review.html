What changed in October

Fixed

  • SEO 02 froze the page after an answer.
  • Team review dropped items and owners.
  • Example numbers showed as the visitor’s own answers once a profile was saved.
  • Industry checks sent ordinary businesses to “Talk to Scott first”.
  • Widget buttons kept saying “ready” after a visitor changed to an answer with gaps.
  • Text contrast, keyboard focus, the mobile menu and the roadmap’s empty state.

Aligned

  • The journey map uses Notice Different, Align to Genuine and Recommend Delight, with Purchase as the pivot.
